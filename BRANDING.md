## License scope

The repository's original code and documentation follow
[CC BY 4.0](LICENSE), whose **Section 3(a)** requires attribution when sharing
licensed material, including modified versions. The attribution information
is in [README.md](README.md); the unmodified standard license text is in
[LICENSE](LICENSE).

MyScoutee logos and brand artwork, if present in source, examples or
documentation, are excluded from CC BY 4.0. Their copyright is retained by the
MyScoutee rights holder. The display permissions below apply only to those
branding assets; they do not modify the code and documentation license.

## Permitted logo display

You may copy and display the unaltered MyScoutee logo:

- To demonstrate or document the original MyScoutee project.
- In a clearly labeled credit or attribution section acknowledging MyScoutee
  as the source of reused work, including in commercial projects.

When displaying the logo, identify it as MyScoutee and link to the
[source repository](https://github.com/fssrepository/myscoutee-template).
Proportional resizing is allowed. Preserve these branding permissions when
redistributing the logo.

## Your project's identity

The MyScoutee logo in template source, examples or UI is demonstration
branding. Remove or replace it when adapting a README header or building
another project's application headers, icons, landing pages and other places
that identify your product.

These permissions do not allow using the logo as your own product identity,
altering it to create your own brand, impersonating MyScoutee, or implying an
official relationship, sponsorship or endorsement. A separate source-credit
section may retain the logo under the permitted-display rules above.

No general trademark license is granted. Other uses of MyScoutee brand artwork
require prior permission from its rights holder.

## Required credit does not require a logo

Attribution for shared code and documentation is required by [LICENSE](LICENSE),
Section 3(a). You can meet it with a text credit and the required source,
license, copyright, disclaimer and modification information. Displaying the
logo is optional and does not replace any of those requirements.
